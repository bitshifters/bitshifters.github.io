Games brought to the BBC Micro and Master from other machines, and BBC games reworked, with the help of AI coding tools. They're by Bitshifters and others in the community, mostly found on [Stardot](https://stardot.org.uk/forums/).

**Play** runs the disc in [jsbeeb](https://bbc.xania.org/) where its host allows. Know of one we've missed? Let us know on Stardot or the Bitshifters Discord.
