Forty-eight palettes of the BBC Micro

The Beeb had eight colours and, in its four-colour modes, room for four of them at a time. That is 70 possible sets. Across 1287 screenshots from the games archive, only 48 were ever used.

Each set below is named for what it looks like and for what turned out to be sitting inside it. Palettes are ordered by how many games chose them.
